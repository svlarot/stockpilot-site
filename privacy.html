Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 May 2026

StockPilot is a phone app that helps small food businesses run their kitchen — inventory, recipes, sales, alerts, and reports. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how to reach us.

What we collect

Account information

When you sign in, we store your email address and a randomly generated account id. We do not ask for your name, phone number, or birthday.

Your business data

When you use StockPilot, you create ingredients, menus, sales, and movement records. We store these on your device. If you turn on cloud sync (a Premium feature), we also store an encrypted copy on our backend so your other devices can see the same numbers.

Receipt photos

If you use AI receipt scanning, your phone runs Google ML Kit text recognition on the device itself. The receipt photo does not leave your device for OCR. We don't upload the photo to our backend, and we don't keep a copy.

Device information

We record a device id (a random string) so multi-device sync can tell your phone from your tablet. The app records device platform (Android) and the optional label or staff name you set. We do not collect IMEI, MAC address, advertising id, or other hardware fingerprints.

What we do not collect

We do not currently use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services. No event telemetry. No behavioural tracking. The app does not phone home with usage data of any kind today; if that changes in a future version we will update this policy and tell you what changed.

Third-party services we use

  • Supabase stores your synced business data (when cloud sync is on). Data is encrypted in transit. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Google AdMob serves ads in the free tier. Google collects its own data per Google's ad policy. The first time the app opens, you'll see a Google consent dialog (UMP) to control personalised ads.
  • Google ML Kit runs receipt text recognition on your device. Per Google's ML Kit terms, no image leaves the device for this feature.
  • Google Play Services is the foundational layer your Android phone provides.
  • Google Play Billing (when subscription tiers become available) processes subscription payments. Today the app does not charge users; Premium is enabled server-side by us.

Why we collect what we collect

  • Account information: to sign you in across devices.
  • Business data: because it is the app — without it the app has nothing to show.
  • Cloud sync data: so a sale recorded on your counter tablet appears on your phone.
  • Device id: so multi-device sync can route changes to the right device.

How long we keep your data

We keep your synced data as long as your account exists. If you delete your account (instructions below), we delete the rows belonging to that account from our database within 30 days. Local data on your device is wiped when you uninstall the app. Backups you have exported to your own files or cloud drive are yours to manage.

Deleting your account

Email support@bluenex.org with the subject "Account deletion" and the email address you signed in with. We will confirm and delete the data within 30 days.

Children's privacy

StockPilot is built for adult business owners and their staff. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, please contact us and we will remove their data.

Security

We use HTTPS for every network request. Supabase storage uses row-level security so only your account can read your rows. We follow accepted practices but we cannot guarantee perfect security — please use a strong password and don't share your account.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially, we will update the "Last updated" date and notify users on the app's next launch. Continued use after the update means you accept the change.

Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or anything else: support@bluenex.org.
